# What's New — v0.4.0

## ⚡ Live PVP (beta) — new tab

Real-time battles against players who are online right now.

- **30 seconds per turn.** A bar counts down; it turns red in the last 10.
- Miss a turn and **the server plays it for you** — the match keeps
  moving instead of stalling.
- Miss **three in a row** and you forfeit.
- You can only challenge people who are actually online.
- Winner takes 500 crystals and XP.

Challenges arrive instantly and expire after 60 seconds. Pick a battle
team on the Battles tab first — it must be 4 different species.

This is separate from the existing Battles tab, which still works
exactly as before.

## Pilot Levels & Re-Boot

Almost everything you do now earns XP: catching, hatching, winning
battles, trading, evolving.

Levels 0 to 20, with rewards at 5, 10, 15 and 20. Level 20 unlocks
**Re-Boot**.

Re-Boot is deliberately brutal. It **destroys every droid you own, all
your crystals and all your materials**. You keep your Dex, your
achievements, your cosmetics, your Pad Level and your workshop slots. In
return you get a permanent bonus — crystal generation on odd Re-Boots,
material rewards on even ones — up to +10% of each across 10 Re-Boots.

You start again with 10,000 crystals and a few materials. There are two
confirmations, and the second makes you type REBOOT.

## Cosmetic Sets

Ten sets, four pieces each. Head and Body give HP; Arms and Legs give
Attack. Common +2% per piece up to Galactic +30%.

Wear all four pieces of one set for a further **+5% to both**.

The two Galactic sets — Astralis and Singularity — can be owned any
time, but can only be **equipped after 5 Re-Boots**.

## Droid Attachments

Three slots per droid: one Mod Chip, one USB Dongle, one Energy Bottle.
Fifteen attachments in total, each focused on one thing: HP, Attack,
Special, Crystal gain or battle Rewards.

Attachment HP and Attack apply only to the droid wearing them. Crystal
bonuses apply to your whole account.

## Online indicators

Friends and guild lists now show a coloured dot: green for online, amber
for idle, grey for offline, with a "last seen" time.

## Active Buffs panel

New panel on the Player tab showing every bonus you have, where it came
from, and whether you've hit the cap on it.

Buffs stack multiplicatively across sources with a ceiling on each type,
so late-game bonuses stay meaningful without breaking the balance.

## Notes

- Live PVP is beta. On Render's free tier the server sleeps when nobody
  is using it, which will interrupt a live match until someone wakes it.
- Attachments and cosmetics don't drop from the Depot yet — that comes
  with the next update.
