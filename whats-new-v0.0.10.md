# Sparkfield — What's New in 0.0.10

The game's name changed too: **Droid Catcher is now Sparkfield.**
Everything below is new since the last update.

---

## Shop (Inventory tab)

Buy materials directly with crystals — Paint, Nova Chips, Beacons, and
the new Augment Core (see below). Prices are deliberately heavy: this
is a shortcut for players who can farm big crystal amounts, not a
replacement for earning materials the normal way. Beta Crown purchase
also moved here, out of the Player tab.

## Outfits

Four purchasable outfits — Earthy, Technology, Wildlife, Funky — 5000✦
each, bought from the Shop. Everyone starts with a free **Basic**
outfit already equipped. Swap between any outfit you own from the
Player tab.

## New Companion: The Enforcer

A third Cosmic-tier companion alongside StarSprite and Nebulfox.
+100% battle damage — same activation pattern as Nebulfox (1 hour
active, then an 8-hour cooldown, tracked per droid you own).

**Heads up:** this buff has no effect yet — there's no battle system
in the game for "damage" to matter in. The Enforcer is built and ready
for when that lands; for now it's just an early collectible.

## New Material: Augment Core

Drops from Depot visits (10% chance per visit), or buy it directly
from the Shop. Intended to customize an individual droid's HP/Attack —
same situation as The Enforcer: that spending mechanic isn't built
yet, so for now Augment Cores simply accumulate for when it is.

## Depot payouts adjusted slightly

Paint and Nova Chip odds per visit trimmed a little (15% → 12% each)
to make room for Augment Core's new 10% chance, so the Depot doesn't
get more generous overall just because there's a fourth possible drop.

## 20 new Football-themed droids

A brand new set, Light and Dark, Common through Legendary. Unlike
every other collection, these only spawn in narrow windows: **Light
side 3pm-5pm, Dark side 8pm-10pm, Saturdays and Sundays only.** They
live in your **main Dex**, not a separate one — deliberately, so full
Dex completion stays a longer-term goal rather than something that
gets finished too quickly.

## Scanning has a small speed limit now

Scan faster than roughly once every 2 seconds and you'll get a brief
"Scanning too fast" message. Normal play isn't affected at all — this
is just a guard against accidental or scripted spam-scanning.

## User Guide now linked in-game

The Player tab has a 📖 **User Guide** link that opens the full guide
directly from the game server — no separate file needed.

## Art support added (art is still being added over time)

The game can now show custom images for: outfits, the Factory egg
icon, a background behind the capture minigame, alignment-based
backgrounds on radar spawn cards, and an animation on active Farm
slots. If you don't see custom art in these spots yet, that's
expected — placeholders and fallbacks are the intended look until real
art gets dropped in behind the scenes. Nothing is broken.

---

*Not part of this update: PVE Battles, Repair Kits, and the
Buddy/Mastery system are all still on the roadmap, not live yet.*
