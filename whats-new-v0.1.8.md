# Sparkfield — What's New in 0.1.8

## Dex reorganized by group
The main Dex now works like the Warehouse — grouped, not one long
list. Groups are buttons (3-4 per line), hidden until tapped:

**Mythical, Nature, WildDroids, Football, Void Zombie, Lumen
Sentinel, Cosmic, Titan** — "wildcard" is now shown as "WildDroids"
(display only, nothing internal changed). Anything that doesn't
cleanly fit a named group falls into an "Others" catch-all for later
refinement.

Event collections (Solar, Superhero) are unaffected — they stay in
their own separate Event Dex, since those are hand-triggered, timed
spawns rather than part of the always-on collection.
