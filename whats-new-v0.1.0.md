# Sparkfield — What's New in 0.1.0

The first of the four major post-0.0.12 extensions: a full Battle
Engine, built PVP-first per your priority, with PVE Titans as a second
content type on the same engine rather than a separate system.

---

## Battle Engine

**PVP** — challenge another player by ID, they see it next time they
check their Battles tab, accept or decline. Once accepted, strict
alternating turns: attack, wait for your opponent to respond, repeat.
4-droid rotating teams — when your active droid faints, the next
non-fainted one swaps in automatically. Win, and you get a crystal
reward.

**Solo Titan Encounters** — 1000✦ entry, 2-hour cooldown, your team of
4 against the Titan alone. Win or lose, you walk away with something —
Energy Tubes either way (more on a win), plus the full reward set if
you win.

**Group Titan Encounters** — invite up to 9 others (10 total). Everyone
who joins pays their own entry fee and turns rotate through the whole
group against one shared Titan. A participant whose team fully faints
is eliminated and skipped in the rotation, but stays in the fight's
history. Win as a group and *everyone still standing* gets their own
full individual reward — this isn't split, everyone who showed up gets
rewarded properly.

**Repair Kits** — heal a fainted droid back to full HP. For now, the
Shop is the only source (1500✦) — the real intended source, Titan
battle rewards, is already live for that specific reward, but a
standalone Repair Kit purchase option exists too until other sources
are added.

## Scaffitan — the Titan

- Winning a Titan fight has a rare chance (8%, our own placeholder
  rate) to also grant you a captured Scaffitan.
- Scaffitan masters through 5 tiers — Common → Uncommon → Rare →
  Legendary → Galactic — spending Energy Tubes (15 → 40 → 75 → 150).
  It also levels normally like any other droid, in parallel.
- At Galactic, it's meaningfully more powerful than any Legendary droid
  and is the first droid to reach the new Galactic rarity tier.
- Catch a duplicate Scaffitan and release it for Energy Tubes instead
  of the usual crystal refund (which would always be worthless for a
  battle-won droid) — 1 to 5 Tubes, weighted so 1 is most common.
- Common/Uncommon Scaffitan can never be swept up by the
  auto-release-duplicates toggle — Scaffitan never enters the normal
  capture flow that feature watches, so this is true by design, not a
  special-cased exception.

## Where things stand
- Scaffitan's Galactic-tier ability (+20% HP, intended for Depot use)
  is defined but inert — the Depot/Galactic system it plugs into is
  still on the roadmap, not built yet.
- The Titan's combat stats and exact "rare" capture percentage are our
  own placeholder numbers — easy to retune once you've seen it in play.
