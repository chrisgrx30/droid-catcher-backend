# What's New — v0.5.3

## Cosmetics and Attachments now actually drop

Both systems were fully built but nothing in the game granted them, so
all 40 cosmetic pieces and 15 attachments were unobtainable. **The Depot
now drops both.**

- **Cosmetic piece:** roughly 1 visit in 12
- **Attachment:** roughly 1 visit in 14

Cosmetic drops are weighted by rarity — but if you already own the piece
that gets rolled, the game gives you an unowned piece of that same
rarity instead. Your odds of a Legendary are unchanged; the drop just
isn't wasted. Only when you own every piece of that rarity do you get a
genuine duplicate, and it says so.

Collecting all 32 non-Galactic pieces takes roughly 1,600 Depot visits.
The full 40 including the two Galactic sets takes around 5,400.

## You can finally equip attachments

There was nowhere to assign them. Tap any droid's stats and you'll now
find three slots — Mod Chip, USB Dongle, Energy Bottle — with a dropdown
of whatever you have spare. Equipping one applies its buff immediately.

## Buddy assignment moved somewhere sensible

Setting a Mastery Buddy is now a button on the droid itself, on any
level 20 droid. Removing a buddy banks its mastery progress rather than
losing it — re-assign later and it picks up where it left off.

## Brood Chamber (from v0.5.2)

Merge two droids into an Astral Brood egg. Both parents are destroyed.
Genesis Bays hold the eggs while they incubate: 1 hour for a Common,
up to 3 days for a Legendary.

The result is never worse than the lower-rarity parent, and two of the
same rarity guarantee at least that rarity. Astralmatron — the Galactic
queen — requires every other Astral Brood droid in your Dex first.

## Notes

- Attachment HP and Attack apply only to the droid wearing them.
  Crystal-gain attachments apply to your whole account.
- Galactic cosmetic sets still need 5 Re-Boots before they can be worn.
