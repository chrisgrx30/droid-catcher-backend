# Sparkfield — What's New in 0.1.3

## Balance
- Titan HP tripled (400 → 1200) — solo Cosmic-tier teams were beating
  it too easily. Verified the actual math: a solo Cosmic team now
  clearly loses (480 vs 1200 needed), Legendary loses narrowly (1120
  vs 1200), Galactic wins comfortably (2400 vs 1200).

## Shop
- Buy buttons simplified to just "Buy — price," no repeated item name.

## Cosmetics — new system
- The Outfit box now sits alongside 4 new slots: Head, Body, Arms,
  Legs. Body/Arms/Legs are placeholders for now ("Coming soon") — Head
  is live, and the Beta Crown is its first equippable item.

## Friends — new feature
- Add a friend by Player ID, they get a request to accept or decline,
  and your friends list shows their name and Dex progress.

## Player tab
- PIN fields back to a single line (Current, New, Update), with New
  Pilot (reset) as a proper button below.

## Everywhere
- Repair now shows a confirmation popup on success (previously
  silent in both places it can be triggered from).
- 4 more panels support custom backgrounds: Guild Leaderboard, Guild
  Chat, Choose Your Battle Team, Your Battle Team.
