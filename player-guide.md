# Sparkfield — Player Guide (Beta)

Welcome to the beta! This covers everything currently in the game.

## What's New in 0.0.9 — Field Ops

A whole new tab, **Field Ops**, with three new ways to earn materials
and droids:

- **Depot** — a ring-timing minigame you can play once an hour (100✦
  per attempt). How close you stop the pulse ring to the target ring
  decides how good your crystal payout is (50-200✦) — but Paint and
  Nova Chip odds stay the same no matter how well you play.
- **Factory** — a bigger commitment: buy Processor slots (up to 5, each
  pricier than the last), win an egg from the same ring minigame,
  assign it to a slot and pay to start a 20-hour incubation, then
  collect. What hatches is a straight rarity roll — skill gets you the
  egg, not the quality of what's inside. You can crush an unwanted egg
  (before or during incubation) for a small chance at a Nova Chip.
- **Beacons** — a consumable you can buy and activate: for 30 minutes,
  your scans have noticeably better odds of turning up something Rare
  or better. Since spawns are shared once they appear, anyone else
  nearby benefits too.

Also: keep an eye out for short, intense spawn bursts — sometimes
Legendaries, sometimes companions — that come and go in just 10
minutes. Nothing you can trigger yourself, but worth jumping on when
you spot one.

## Navigating the App

The game is organized into tabs across the top:

| Tab | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Player** | Login, your stats, Control Pad upgrades, Cosmetics |
| **Capture** | The radar — scan for droids and run the capture minigame |
| **Farm** | Crystal balance, totals, Collect button, Workshop slots (with droids shown inline) |
| **Companion** | Your equipped companion, its buff, activation status, and any others you own |
| **Storage** | Droids not currently farming — level up, evolve, release, assign, or tap for full stats |
| **Field Ops** | Depot, Factory/Processors, and Beacons — see above |
| **Guilds** | Create/join a guild, see members, chat, and the Dex leaderboard |
| **Inventory** | Crystals, Paint, Nova Chips, and redeeming promo codes |
| **Trading** | Trading and the public Wishlist board |
| **Dex** | Your collection progress, plus the separate Event Dex |

Tap any tab to switch — your data stays loaded, nothing resets.

## Getting Started

**1. Deploy your pilot** (Player tab). Enter a callsign (username) and a
4-8 digit PIN, then hit "Deploy / Resume Pilot."
- New callsign → creates a fresh pilot.
- Existing callsign + matching PIN → resumes that pilot, even on a
  different phone or browser. Your PIN is how you log back in anywhere.
- Pick a PIN you'll remember — there's no "forgot PIN" recovery yet.

**2. Choose your starter droid.** You'll be offered a free Common-tier
droid — pick Light or Dark, whichever collection appeals to you. It
starts farming crystals immediately, no capturing required.

**3. Scan for droids** (Capture tab). Hit "Use My Location & Scan" — this
uses your phone/browser's real GPS location to find droids nearby.
You'll need to allow location access when prompted.

## The Core Loop

Droid Catcher runs on one loop: **capture droids → they farm crystals for
you → spend crystals to capture/upgrade more droids.**

- Droids you assign to a **Workshop Slot** (Farm tab) passively generate
  crystals over time (shown as crystals/minute).
- Crystals are the only currency for almost everything: capturing,
  leveling, unlocking slots, upgrading your pad, evolving droids, and
  buying cosmetics.
- **Watch your crystal balance** — the Control Pad literally needs
  crystals to function. You can't attempt a capture with zero.

## Capturing Droids

On the **Capture** tab, tap any droid on the radar to open its capture
panel.

**The LOCK-ON minigame:** a marker sweeps back and forth across a bar. A
highlighted "zone" marks your target window — the closer to the center
you stop the marker, the higher your accuracy. Rarer droids have a
narrower zone (harder to time), which is why Legendaries are a real test
of skill, not just luck.

**Crystals matter too:** each droid has a minimum crystal cost just to
attempt it (scales with rarity — Commons are cheap, Legendaries and the
companion tier are expensive). Spending more than the minimum, up to a
cap, improves your odds further.

**Risk is real:** if the capture fails, your spent crystals are **gone
for good** — there's no refund on a failed attempt. Spend accordingly.

**Critical captures:** upgrading your Control Pad gives you a chance at
a "critical" capture — a guaranteed success regardless of the odds. Rare
early on, more common as you invest in pad upgrades.

**After every attempt**, a popup tells you exactly what happened —
captured or broke free, whether it was a critical, any Paint you picked
up, and (on a failure) a reminder of how many crystals you just lost.

## Droid Types

**Alignment:** every droid is Light ☀, Dark 🌑, or the ultra-rare Cosmic
✦ (companions only). Light droids spawn more often during the day, Dark
droids more often at night — based on the real time of day wherever
you're standing.

**Collections:** droids come from three regular themed sets — **Mythical**
(fairies, foxes, ravens, dragons), **Nature / Corrupted Nature**
(saplings, vines, fungus, world trees), and **Wildcard** — a grab-bag of
everything else. There's also **Solar**, which only exists during the
Summer Event (see above). Purely thematic, but useful to know for
Dex-hunting and event boosts.

**Rarity:** Common → Uncommon → Rare → Legendary → Cosmic (companions
only). Higher rarity means lower capture odds, higher minimum crystal
cost, but much stronger crystal production.

## Variants — The Shiny Hunt

Any droid you capture, regardless of species or rarity, has a small
chance of coming up as a special variant. This is visible on the radar
*before* you even attempt the capture, so keep an eye out:

- **Platinum** — pristine chrome finish, massively boosted crystal
  production (5x normal).
- **Rusty** — weathered finish, a solid crystal boost (2x normal), and
  can be evolved further (see below).
- **Funky** — an evolved form of Rusty (see Paint below): a chosen
  primary color and an even bigger boost (3.5x normal).

Your Dex tracks which variants you've caught per species — see the Icon
Glossary above for what the dots mean. The radar's 🎮 icon is
variant-specific too — catching a standard Puffkin won't hide a Rusty
Puffkin spawn as "already caught."

---

## Paint & Funky Evolution

Any successful capture has a small chance to drop **Paint** — a banked
currency, not tied to any specific droid, shown on the Inventory tab.
The Depot also has a flat chance of dropping Paint on every visit.
Once you've saved up enough, spend it (from the Storage tab, on any
Rusty droid you own) to evolve it into **Funky**: pick a primary color
(red, yellow, or blue) and enjoy the upgraded crystal rate.

---

## Species Evolution

Some species can evolve into stronger forms using **Nova Chips** (shown
on the Inventory tab) — a currency you get by **releasing droids**,
from Depot visits, or from crushing an unwanted Factory egg. Evolving
keeps your droid's level and variant, it just becomes a new (better)
species going forward. On the Storage tab, a droid eligible to evolve
shows an "Evolve →" button with its target species and Nova Chip cost.

---

## Releasing Droids

Don't need a droid anymore? On the Storage tab, release it for scrap:
you get back 1.5x whatever it originally cost you to capture, plus a
chance at a Nova Chip. A popup confirms exactly what you got.

**Releasing more than one at a time:** tick the checkbox on each droid
you want gone (or hit "Select All"), then "Release Selected" for one
combined result.

(Starter droids and hatched Prototypes were free, so releasing one
refunds nothing — but can still drop a Nova Chip.)

## The Farm Tab

- **Slots:** you start with 1 unlocked slot; there are 10 total. Each
  additional slot costs more crystals to unlock than the last.
- Every farming droid shows right in its slot — species, level, current
  crystal rate, and its variant if it has one. **Tap a slot** to expand
  it and either level up that droid or unassign it.
- **Total Rate** shows your combined crystals/min, and **Active Buff**
  summarizes whatever companion effect is currently boosting you.
- To put a droid *into* a slot, that happens from the **Storage tab**.

## The Storage Tab

- Only shows droids that **aren't** currently farming.
- Sorted by rarity, then species, then variant.
- **Tap a droid's icon or name** for its full stat breakdown.
- Level up, evolve, release, or assign to a slot — all from the same row.
- **HP / Attack:** groundwork for a future battle system — no effect yet.

## The Field Ops Tab

**Depot** — hit "Visit Depot" to spend 100✦ and play the ring minigame.
A pulse ring grows outward from the center; tap STOP as close as you
can to the static target ring. Land inside it for a good payout,
overshoot badly for a small one — but you always get *something*.
1-hour cooldown after each visit.

**Beacon** — buy one for 300✦, hit Activate, and for 30 minutes your
scans lean noticeably rarer. Only one active at a time.

**Factory** — this one's a bigger loop:
1. Buy a Processor slot (up to 5 — each one costs more than the last).
2. Play the same ring minigame (100✦) to try winning an egg. Here,
   accuracy only decides hit-or-miss — a miss costs the crystals but
   doesn't touch your cooldown, so retry right away. A hit gives you an
   egg and starts a 3-hour cooldown before your next attempt.
3. Your unassigned eggs show in a list — assign one to any empty
   Processor slot and pay 100✦ to start a 20-hour incubation.
4. Once it's ready, **Collect** — the species and rarity are a fixed
   roll (Legendary 5% / Rare 15% / Uncommon 30% / Common ~50% / the
   ultra-rare Cosmic companion tier 0.01%), completely independent of
   how well you played the minigame. The droid lands straight in
   Storage.
5. Not feeling an egg? **Crush** it (unassigned or mid-incubation) for
   a small chance at a Nova Chip instead. Crushing mid-incubation
   doesn't refund the crystals you already spent starting it.

## Control Pad Upgrades

On the Player tab — separate from droid leveling, this upgrades your
capture device itself. Spending crystals here raises your
critical-capture chance and slightly widens your accuracy-skill ceiling
in the LOCK-ON minigame. Account-wide, not per-droid.

## Companion Droids

Two Cosmic-tier companions exist, both rarer than Legendary — equip one
(on the **Companion** tab) for a passive pilot-wide buff. Only one
companion can be equipped at a time, but you can swap freely between any
you've captured.

- **StarSprite** — +50% to your *total* crystal production. Always
  active the moment it's equipped, nothing to activate.
- **Nebulfox** — +100% capture success chance. This one needs to be
  **activated**: hit the Activate button on the Companion tab, and it
  runs for **1 hour**, then goes on an **8-hour cooldown** before it can
  be activated again. That cooldown is tracked per droid — if you own
  two Nebulfoxes, you can equip and activate the second the moment the
  first goes on cooldown.

## Time-Exclusive Events

Occasionally a limited-time event boosts spawn rates for a specific
collection or rarity tier — keep an eye on the event banner above the
radar on the Capture tab for whatever's currently live, including how
long it has left. Some run for hours, some for just a sharp 10-minute
burst.

The Summer Event (top of this guide) is the biggest one so far — it
makes 8 entirely new Solar-collection droids spawn that otherwise never
appear at all.

## Trading

On the Trading tab — propose a trade with another player using their
Player ID: offer any of your droids, and optionally request a droid
and/or crystals back.
- A freshly captured (or freshly traded) droid has a short cooldown
  before it can be traded again.
- Whoever *receives* a droid in a trade pays a small crystal fee, scaled
  by rarity.
- You don't have to request anything back — a trade can be a pure gift,
  or you can ask for crystals only.

## Wishlist

Also on the Trading tab — post what you're looking for (a specific
droid, optionally with a variant preference, or Paint with a color
preference) and it goes up on a board **every player can see**, along
with your Player ID. Anyone can offer you a trade straight from your
listing — accepting it automatically clears your wish off the board.

Paint wishes show on the board for visibility but aren't
auto-fulfillable through a trade yet — coordinate directly with that
player for now.

## Guilds

On the Guilds tab — create or join a small guild with friends (up to 12
members).

- **Members list** shows everyone, with a 👑 next to the creator.
- **Dex Leaderboard** ranks your guild by Dex completion.
- **Chat** — note this refreshes when you open or revisit the tab, it
  isn't instant like a normal messaging app.
- The guild creator can **kick** a member if needed. If you're kicked:
  you can join a *different* guild after 24 hours, but can't rejoin
  *that same* guild for 30 days.

## Cosmetics

On the Player tab — purely for bragging rights. Currently available: the
**Beta Crown** (1000 crystals) — no gameplay effect, just shows you were
here for the beta.

---

## Redeem Codes

On the Inventory tab — enter a promo code for a one-time reward
(crystals, Paint, Nova Chips, and/or a specific droid). Each code can
only be redeemed once per player.

---

## The Dex

On the Dex tab — every species you've ever caught shows up, with three
small dots showing which variants (Rusty, Platinum, Funky) you've
caught, and a small "e" badge if that species is evolution-only. A
species stays "caught" even if you later trade or release the droid.
Evolved species sit right next to their original form.

**Event Dex:** a separate section for Summer-Event-exclusive species —
these don't count toward your main completion percentage.

## Quick Tips

- Don't spend crystals below a droid's minimum requirement — it'll just
  get rejected, no crystals lost.
- Failed captures still cost you the crystals you spent.
- Level up before you release — refunds are based on capture cost, not
  current level, so there's no rush to release early.
- Nebulfox's buff is powerful but time-limited — activate it right
  before a serious hunting session, not the moment you get it.
- Check the radar during both day and night for a different mix of
  Light vs. Dark droids.
- A Beacon is most worth activating right before a longer hunting
  session, not idly — the 30 minutes ticks down whether you're
  scanning or not.
- Factory eggs are worth crushing early if you're low on Processor
  slots and already have several waiting — an idle egg does nothing
  for you.
- Post to the Wishlist early for anything you're missing.
