# Droid Catcher — Player Guide (Beta)

Welcome to the beta! This covers everything currently in the game. Some
things will change based on what we learn from this test — if something
feels off or confusing, that's exactly the kind of feedback we want.

---

## What's Changed Recently

- The app is now split into **tabs** instead of one long scrolling page
  (see "Navigating the App" below) — much less scrolling.
- **Log in from any device** — pick a callsign and PIN once, use them
  anywhere.
- **A new companion, Nebulfox** — boosts your capture success chance
  instead of your crystal farm.
- **Popup after every capture attempt** — shows exactly what happened
  (captured / broke free, any Paint dropped, crystals lost) instead of
  only logging it quietly.
- **Already-caught indicator on the radar** — a small 🎮 icon shows on
  any spawn whose species you've already logged in your Dex.
- **Dex improvements** — evolved species (like Bushy) now sit right next
  to their base form (Leafkin), and there are three small colored dots
  per species showing which special variants you've caught.
- **Release droids for scrap** — get some crystals back (and a chance at
  a Nova Chip) instead of a droid just sitting unused forever.
- **Species evolution and Funky (painted) droids** — see their own
  sections below.
- **Guild member lists** — you can now see who's actually in your guild.
- **Inventory tab** — Paint and Nova Chips now have a proper place to be
  seen, instead of being invisible numbers.
- Capture difficulty on Common/Uncommon droids was tightened slightly —
  early feedback was that they were a bit too easy.

## Navigating the App

The game is organized into tabs across the top:

| Tab | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Player** | Login, your stats, Control Pad upgrades, Cosmetics |
| **Capture** | The radar — scan for droids and run the capture minigame |
| **Farm** | Crystal balance, Collect button, Companion slot, Workshop slots |
| **Storage** | Your full owned-droid list — level up, evolve, release, assign |
| **Guilds** | Create/join a guild, see its members |
| **Inventory** | Paint, Nova Chips, and redeeming promo codes |
| **Events & Trading** | Time-exclusive events and player-to-player trading |
| **Dex** | Your collection progress |

Tap any tab to switch — your data stays loaded, nothing resets.

## Getting Started

**1. Deploy your pilot** (Player tab). Enter a callsign (username) and a
4-8 digit PIN, then hit "Deploy / Resume Pilot."
- New callsign → creates a fresh pilot.
- Existing callsign + matching PIN → resumes that pilot, even on a
  different phone or browser. Your PIN is how you log back in anywhere.
- Pick a PIN you'll remember — there's no "forgot PIN" recovery yet.

**2. Choose your starter droid.** You'll be offered a free Common-tier
droid — pick Light or Dark, whichever collection appeals to you. It
starts farming crystals immediately, no capturing required.

**3. Scan for droids** (Capture tab). Hit "Use My Location & Scan" — this
uses your phone/browser's real GPS location to find droids nearby.
You'll need to allow location access when prompted.

## The Core Loop

Droid Catcher runs on one loop: **capture droids → they farm crystals for
you → spend crystals to capture/upgrade more droids.**

- Droids you assign to a **Workshop Slot** (Farm tab) passively generate
  crystals over time (shown as crystals/minute).
- Crystals are the only currency for almost everything: capturing,
  leveling, unlocking slots, upgrading your pad, evolving droids, and
  buying cosmetics.
- **Watch your crystal balance** — the Control Pad literally needs
  crystals to function. You can't attempt a capture with zero.

## Capturing Droids

On the **Capture** tab, tap any droid on the radar to open its capture
panel.

**The LOCK-ON minigame:** a marker sweeps back and forth across a bar. A
highlighted "zone" marks your target window — the closer to the center
you stop the marker, the higher your accuracy. Rarer droids have a
narrower zone (harder to time), which is why Legendaries are a real test
of skill, not just luck.

**Crystals matter too:** each droid has a minimum crystal cost just to
attempt it (scales with rarity — Commons are cheap, Legendaries and the
companion tier are expensive). Spending more than the minimum, up to a
cap, improves your odds further.

**Risk is real:** if the capture fails, your spent crystals are **gone
for good** — there's no refund on a failed attempt. Spend accordingly.

**Critical captures:** upgrading your Control Pad gives you a chance at
a "critical" capture — a guaranteed success regardless of the odds. Rare
early on, more common as you invest in pad upgrades.

**After every attempt**, a popup tells you exactly what happened —
captured or broke free, whether it was a critical, any Paint you picked
up, and (on a failure) a reminder of how many crystals you just lost.

## Droid Types

**Alignment:** every droid is Light ☀, Dark 🌑, or the ultra-rare Cosmic
✦ (companions only). Light droids spawn more often during the day, Dark
droids more often at night — based on the real time of day wherever
you're standing.

**Collections:** droids come from two themed sets — **Mythical**
(fairies, foxes, ravens, dragons) and **Nature / Corrupted Nature**
(saplings, vines, fungus, world trees). Purely thematic, but useful to
know for Dex-hunting and event boosts.

**Rarity:** Common → Uncommon → Rare → Legendary → Cosmic (companions
only). Higher rarity means lower capture odds, higher minimum crystal
cost, but much stronger crystal production.

## Icon Glossary

A quick reference for every symbol you'll see around the app:

| Icon | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ☀ | Light alignment |
| 🌑 | Dark alignment |
| ✦ | Cosmic alignment (companion droids only) |
| 🎮 | This species is already in your Dex — seen on the radar, for Dex-completion hunters |
| ✦ (next to a number) | Crystals — the main currency |
| 🎨 | Paint — spend on a Rusty droid to evolve it to Funky |
| 🔷 | Nova Chips — spend to evolve certain species (e.g. Leafkin → Bushy) |
| 👑 | Marks the creator of a guild, next to their name in the member list |

| Small dots under a Dex entry | Left-to-right: Rusty, Platinum, Funky caught status for that species — lit up in color once caught, dim if not |

## Variants — The Shiny Hunt

Any droid you capture, regardless of species or rarity, has a small
chance of coming up as a special variant. This is visible on the radar
*before* you even attempt the capture, so keep an eye out:

- **Platinum** — pristine chrome finish, massively boosted crystal
  production (5x normal).
- **Rusty** — weathered finish, a solid crystal boost (2x normal), and
  can be evolved further (see below).
- **Funky** — an evolved form of Rusty (see Paint below): a chosen
  primary color and an even bigger boost (3.5x normal).

Your Dex tracks which variants you've caught per species — see the Icon
Glossary above for what the dots mean.

---

## Paint & Funky Evolution

Any successful capture has a small chance to drop **Paint** — a banked
currency, not tied to any specific droid, shown on the Inventory tab.
Once you've saved up enough, spend it (from the Storage tab, on any
Rusty droid you own) to evolve it into **Funky**: pick a primary color
(red, yellow, or blue) and enjoy the upgraded crystal rate. That color
choice is also what shows up in your Dex's Funky dot for that species.

---

## Species Evolution

Some species can evolve into stronger forms using **Nova Chips** (shown
on the Inventory tab) — a separate currency you get by **releasing
droids**, not from capturing. Evolving keeps your droid's level and
variant, it just becomes a new (better) species going forward. On the
Storage tab, a droid eligible to evolve shows an "Evolve →" button with
its target species and Nova Chip cost. Once you've evolved into a new
species, it'll show up right next to its original form in your Dex.

---

## Releasing Droids

Don't need a droid anymore? On the Storage tab, release it for scrap:
you get back 1.5x whatever it originally cost you to capture, plus a
chance at a Nova Chip. This can't be undone, so double-check before
confirming.

(Starter droids were free, so releasing one refunds nothing — but can
still drop a Nova Chip.)

## The Farm & Storage Tabs

**Farm tab:**
- **Slots:** you start with 1 unlocked slot; there are 10 total. Each
  additional slot costs more crystals to unlock than the last. A droid
  only farms crystals while assigned to an unlocked slot.
- **Companion:** see below — equip one here for a passive buff.

**Storage tab:**
- Your full droid list, sorted with farming droids first, then everyone
  else grouped by rarity, species, and variant.
- **Leveling:** spend crystals to level up an individual droid — boosts
  its crystal production, HP, and Attack. Higher-rarity droids cost more
  crystals per level than common ones.
- **HP / Attack:** every droid has these stats, scaling with level. They
  don't do anything yet — they're groundwork for a future battle system.

---

## Control Pad Upgrades

On the Player tab — separate from droid leveling, this upgrades your
capture device itself. Spending crystals here raises your
critical-capture chance and slightly widens your accuracy-skill ceiling
in the LOCK-ON minigame. This is account-wide, not per-droid.

---

## Companion Droids

Two Cosmic-tier companions exist — both rarer than Legendary, with even
rarer odds of a Platinum/Rusty variant. Neither farms crystals itself;
instead, whichever one you equip (on the Farm tab) gives your whole
pilot a passive buff. Only one companion can be equipped at a time, but
you can swap freely between any you've captured.

- **StarSprite** — +50% to your *total* crystal production from everything else.
- **Nebulfox** — +100% capture success chance on every attempt. Great for
  finally landing that Legendary you've been missing.

---

## Time-Exclusive Events

On the Events & Trading tab — occasionally a limited-time event will
boost spawn rates for a specific collection (e.g. "Nature Festival"
makes Nature-collection droids much more common for a few hours). Keep
an eye on the event banner — these come and go.

---

## Trading

Also on the Events & Trading tab — propose a trade with another player
using their Player ID: offer any of your droids and/or crystals, request
theirs in return. A few rules to know:
- A freshly captured (or freshly traded) droid has a short cooldown
  before it can be traded again.
- Whoever *receives* a droid in a trade pays a small crystal fee, scaled
  by rarity — trading is a convenience, not a free shortcut around
  capturing rares yourself.

---

## Guilds

On the Guilds tab — create or join a small guild with friends (up to 12
members). You can now see the full member list (with a 👑 next to the
creator). Right now this is just a shared group — no gameplay effects
yet — but it's the foundation for team features we'll build on later.

---

## Cosmetics

On the Player tab — purely for bragging rights. Currently available: the
**Beta Crown** (1000 crystals) — no gameplay effect, just shows you were
here for the beta. More cosmetics will be added over time.

---

## Redeem Codes

On the Inventory tab — if we give you a promo code, enter it there for a
one-time reward (crystals and/or a specific droid). Each code can only
be redeemed once per player.

---

## The Dex

On the Dex tab — every species you've ever caught shows up, with three
small dots underneath showing which variants (Rusty, Platinum, Funky)
you've caught of it — see the Icon Glossary above. A species stays
marked as "caught" even if you later trade or release the droid — the
Dex tracks what you've *achieved*, not what you currently own. Evolved
species now sit right next to their original form for easy reference.

---

## Quick Tips

- Don't spend crystals below a droid's minimum requirement — it'll just
  get rejected, no crystals lost.
- Failed captures still cost you the crystals you spent — factor that
  risk in, especially for expensive Legendary/Cosmic attempts.
- Level up before you release — a higher-level droid released still only
  refunds based on capture cost, not current level, so there's no rush
  to release early for value reasons.
- Companion buff applies to your *whole* farm (or every capture attempt,
  for Nebulfox), so it's often worth prioritizing once you have one.
- Check the radar regularly during both day and night — you'll see a
  noticeably different mix of Light vs. Dark droids.
- Watch for the 🎮 icon on the radar if you're chasing full Dex
  completion — it tells you at a glance whether a spawn is new or
  already logged.

## What's Not in Yet

A few things are planned but intentionally not part of this beta:
- **PVE raid battles** (the HP/Attack stats exist already as groundwork)
- **Guild-vs-guild PVP**
- **Real character art** — droids currently show either your added
  artwork (if provided) or a simple placeholder icon

Thanks for testing — report anything confusing, broken, or just plain
unfun. That's exactly what this round is for.
